About this curriculum

A free, university-style learning plan as a simple website with a progress checklist. It has 4 years, 16 terms of 3 months each, daily class timings, term events, projects and checkpoints, built only from free resources.

Core: software development, machine learning and AIEvery term: English, project management, architecture and system design, product and selling

No frameworks, no build step, no sign-up. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Features

  • 16 terms (13 weeks each: 12 teaching weeks and 1 break week)
  • Every course name is a link to its free resource
  • Tick boxes with a progress bar, saved in your browser
  • A project and six events per term: orientation, proposal, midterm, mid-project review, finals and Demo Day, retrospective
  • A checkpoint with questions for every term
  • A daily timetable, a weekly rhythm and a table of secondary subjects by year
  • Light mode (cream and teal) and dark mode, with a toggle button that remembers your choice
  • Works on phones and desktops

How progress is saved

Ticks are saved in your own browser (localStorage), on that device only. They do not sync between devices and are not shared with anyone. Clearing browser data or using the Clear progress button removes them.

Good to know

  • This plan gives you skills and a public portfolio, not an accredited degree.
  • It assumes about 8 hours of study a day, Monday to Saturday. If you study 4 hours a day, make every term 6 months.
  • Free courses move or change. Check each link before you start a term, and replace broken ones with an updated link (you can keep a list of updated links in your own Google Doc).
  • Train machine learning models on free Google Colab or Kaggle notebooks, not on an old computer.
